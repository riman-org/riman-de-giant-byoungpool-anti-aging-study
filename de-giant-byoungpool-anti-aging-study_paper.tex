\begin{abstract}
Background: The German skincare market demands high tolerance for cold, dry microclimates and rigorous regulatory compliance. This study evaluates the consumer perception and clinical efficacy of RIMAN Skincare’s flagship ingredient, Giant BYoungPool™ (Centella Asiatica), specifically within the context of the Active Cream EX formulation. Methods: A stratified survey of N=200 German consumers across Munich, Berlin, and Hamburg assessed Likert-scale perceptions of texture, absorption, scent, and value, alongside Net Promoter Score (NPS) and MBTI-based behavioral segmentation. Results: The overall composite mean was 3.6/5.0 with a Top-2-Box satisfaction rate of 60.8\%. Notably, 91\% of respondents found the Centella Asiatica Leaf Water base scent non-intrusive, while 82.5\% expressed confidence in INCI labeling compliance. However, trust in specific 'collagen-boosting triterpenoids' claims remained moderate (mean 3.16). Strategic Implications: The data reveals that cost-per-milliliter optimization is the sole driver for upgrading from starter to full sets (100\% selection rate). Furthermore, uncertainty about suitability and complex cancellation policies are primary barriers to subscription conversion. These insights inform RIMAN Mall’s regional strategy for enhancing perceived value through transparent INCELLDERM education and flexible trial mechanisms.

\vspace{0.4em}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} RIMAN Skincare, Giant BYoungPool, INCELLDERM, German Microclimate, Price Sensitivity, Descending INCI
\end{abstract}

\section{1. Formulation Architecture \& Study Methodology}
The study focuses on the Giant BYoungPool™ Active Cream EX, formulated with 57\% pure Korean-grown Giant BYoungPool leaf water. The methodology employed a stratified random sampling of 200 participants across three major German metropolitan areas: Munich (Alpine Cold, 38\% RH), Berlin (Continental, 40\% RH), and Hamburg (Maritime, 62\% RH). Participants were segmented by skin type, income tier, occupation, and MBTI personality profiles to capture nuanced behavioral responses. The core patents evaluated include antioxidant activity (phenols/minerals), soothing/anti-irritant properties, moisturization, collagen-boosting triterpenoids, hyperpigmentation reduction, and wrinkle smoothing. Data collection utilized Likert-5 scales for perceptual attributes and single/multi-choice questions for economic and logistical preferences.
\section{2. Quantitative Metrics \& Microclimate Adaptation}
Quantitative analysis reveals a composite mean score of 3.6 out of 5.0, with an overall Top-2-Box satisfaction percentage of 60.8\%. Regarding microclimate adaptation, 66\% of respondents agreed that the texture is appropriate for Germany's dry, cold climate (Mean: 3.62). Scent acceptability was exceptionally high, with 91\% finding the Centella Asiatica Leaf Water base non-intrusive (Mean: 3.91). Regulatory trust was also strong, with 82.5\% confirming that INCI labeling meets German safety standards (Mean: 3.92). However, absorption speed received a lower rating (Mean: 3.48, Top-2-Box: 48\%), indicating a potential gap between urban morning routine expectations and product performance. Perceived value linked to the '57\% pure leaf water' specification scored 3.66, suggesting that ingredient concentration transparency positively influences valuation.
\section{3. Cross-Demographic, MBTI \& B2B/C-End Stratification}
Demographic stratification highlights distinct preference patterns. Affluent/High Net Worth individuals reported the highest satisfaction (Mean: 3.78), followed by Upper-Middle Income groups (3.67). In terms of skin type, Normal/Dehydrated Urban users showed the highest acceptance (3.69), whereas Oily/Acne-Prone users had the lowest (3.43), suggesting a need for lighter formulations or targeted marketing for this segment. MBTI analysis indicates that ISTP and INFP personalities exhibited higher satisfaction scores (3.75 and 3.74 respectively), aligning with minimalist or values-driven consumption habits. B2B stakeholders, particularly Aesthetic Clinic Owners, rated the product highly (3.77), viewing it as a viable professional-grade solution. Conversely, Work-from-Home Parents showed lower engagement (3.50), likely due to time constraints and budget sensitivity.
\section{4. Regulatory Compliance \& Regional Strategy}
Strategic implications for RIMAN Mall in Germany center on overcoming conversion barriers. The Net Promoter Score (NPS) stands at -15.5, driven by a high proportion of passives (76.5\%) and detractors (19.5\%). Key findings indicate that 100\% of respondents considering an upgrade from starter to full-size rituals do so primarily for 'cost\_per\_ml\_optimization.' Therefore, bundling strategies must emphasize volume discounts. Barriers to subscription include 'uncertainty\_about\_suitability' (100\%), 'complex\_cancellation\_policy' (91.5\%), and 'high\_initial\_commitment\_cost' (89.5\%). To address these, RIMAN should implement flexible trial sizes and simplified return policies. Additionally, since 73\% compare pricing against premium K-beauty imports, maintaining competitive EUR pricing while highlighting INCELLDERM patent-backed benefits like soothing anti-irritant effects (selected by 91\% as critical for repurchase) is essential for market penetration.

\section{Conclusion}
This study confirms that Giant BYoungPool™ Active Cream EX holds significant promise in the German market, particularly regarding its sensory profile and regulatory compliance. However, the negative NPS (-15.5) signals a critical need for improved customer retention strategies. The data suggests that while initial interest is high, conversion to full rituals is hindered by perceived risk and rigid subscription models. By leveraging the 57\% leaf water specification to justify value and addressing the 100\% demand for cost-efficiency in bundles, RIMAN can transform passive users into promoters. Future iterations should focus on optimizing absorption speed for urban routines and providing clearer educational content on triterpenoid efficacy to bridge the trust gap identified in Q04.
